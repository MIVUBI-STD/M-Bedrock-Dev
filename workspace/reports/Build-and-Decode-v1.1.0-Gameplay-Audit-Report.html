3. Multi-arena system

Closed from deterministic evidence

  • 4 logical arenas with +384 X spacing.
  • Single authoritative arena membership.
  • Arena-scoped match identity and ticking-area naming.
  • Worst-case named ticking areas: 5 total (1 lobby + 4 arenas).
  • Generation-bound reset invalidates stale reset work.
  • Shared fill serialization alone does not prove cross-arena corruption.
  • Stale Arena 1 form callback is guarded from mutating a later Arena 2 session.

Still needs deciding proof

  • Physical Arena 2–4 voxel equivalence against Arena 1.
  • Containment / mayfly / build escape.
  • Four arenas at intended maximum occupancy.
  • Four arenas recovering after mixed-phase reload.

4. Connection / recovery system

Closed understanding

  • playerLeave routes into disconnect lifecycle.
  • Offline grace preserves session ownership.
  • Reconnect reconciliation follows expiry handling.
  • Pause/continuation depends on required active-player/team state.
  • Same-player arena migration is guarded against stale callbacks.

Known issue

BUG-BAD-DEPARTED-TEAM-WINNER is connection-adjacent, not a reconnect-core failure. Exit/expiry removes active membership correctly; final winner eligibility later switches to historical participant state.

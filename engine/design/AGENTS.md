# Game Design Agent Rules

- Game Design is map/mode scoped.
- Do not derive design from Minecraft documentation.
- Do not promote generic engineering recommendations into gameplay requirements.
- Reconstructed design must remain distinguishable from authored/approved design.
- A diagnosis may claim a design violation only when the expectation is grounded in approved design or sufficiently strong authored evidence.

- Do not classify an anomaly as a defect when an applicable approved intent rule allows it or an explicit exception applies.
- Missing or unspecified intent resolves to design ambiguity, not an invented expected behavior.
- Balance/UX concerns that match approved intent route to design review, not Bug Report promotion.
- Implementation behavior never becomes Game Design authority merely because it is consistent or repeated.
